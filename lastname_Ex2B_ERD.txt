Reading the diagram:
An Owner can have zero or many Dogs → [0,n] on the Owner side.
Each Dog must belong to exactly one Owner → [1,1] on the Dog side.
A Dog can have zero or many Walks → [0,n].
Each Walk must involve exactly one Dog and exactly one Walker → [1,1] on each.
A Walker can have zero or many Walks → [0,n].
